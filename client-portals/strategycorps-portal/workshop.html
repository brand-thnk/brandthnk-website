Day 1: Get Clear on the Story

Focus

Understand where the business is, where it's going, and how leadership wants to describe that evolution. Get to a shared point of view on the company story and the positioning directions worth pursuing.

Morning
Where the Business Is Today
Open reflection on the current state — what's working, what feels muddy, where the gaps are between how leadership talks about the company internally vs. externally.
Mid-Morning
Where the Business Is Going
The growth story taking shape. What does the next chapter look like, and how does positioning need to carry that forward without getting ahead of what's real today?
Midday
Bezing and MonetizeIQ: The Integration Question
How should these two product lines fit together in the StrategyCorps story? Are they additive, integrated, or sequenced? What does a banker need to understand about how they connect?
Afternoon
What Bankers Need to Hear
A frank look at what language lands with community bank buyers — and what confuses them. What assumptions does the current messaging make? Where does it lose people?
Late Afternoon
Initial Positioning Directions
Emerging directions from the day's conversation. Not final — just enough shape to react to and refine on Day 2.

End of Day 1 Output

A shared point of view on the company story and the main positioning paths worth pursuing on Day 2.

Day 2: Turn the Story Into Usable Language

Focus

Take the best direction from Day 1 and turn it into language the team can actually use. Leave with a clear positioning direction, a working message framework, and practical guidance for sales and marketing.

Morning
Review and Refine the Strongest Direction
Revisit the positioning directions that emerged from Day 1. Which one resonates most? What needs to shift? Get to a single direction the room can commit to.
Mid-Morning
What Should Lead the Story?
A critical choice: does the story lead with the platform, the problem, the outcome, or the use case? Different entry points work for different audiences and moments. We'll decide together.
Midday
The Clearest Banker-Friendly Language
Translate the positioning direction into language a community bank executive can receive without translation. Test multiple framings. Land on what's clearest and most credible.
Afternoon
Proof Points and Use Cases
Identify the strongest evidence behind the positioning. What client examples, outcomes, and use cases make the story concrete? What do we lead with in a sales conversation?
Late Afternoon
How the Story Carries Forward
Align on how this positioning should show up in sales conversations, marketing, and board discussions. What changes immediately? What gets updated over time?
Close
Next Steps
Who owns what. What Allison delivers in the synthesis. What the team starts using right away.

End of Day 2 Output

A clear positioning direction, working message framework, and practical guidance for how the team should start using it. Synthesis document to follow within 10 business days.
