Day 1: Get Clear on the Story

Focus

Figure out what the actual story is. Where the business is, where it's going, and how leadership wants to describe the distance between them.

Morning
Where the Business Is Today
Open reflection on current state. What's working. What feels muddy. Where the internal story and the external story don't match.
Mid-Morning
Where the Business Is Going
The growth story taking shape. What does the next chapter look like — and how does the positioning need to carry that forward without getting ahead of what's real today?
Midday
BaZing and MonetizeIQ: The Integration Question
The hardest question of the day. Are these two product lines additive, integrated, or sequenced — and what does a banker actually need to understand about how they connect?
Afternoon
What Bankers Need to Hear
A frank look at what language lands and what loses people. What assumptions does the current messaging make that buyers don't share?
Late Afternoon
Initial Positioning Directions
Rough directions from the day's conversation — not final, just enough shape to pressure-test on Day 2.

End of Day 1 Output

A shared read on the company story and the positioning paths worth taking into Day 2.

Day 2: Turn the Story Into Usable Language

Focus

Take the strongest direction from Day 1 and make it writable. Leave with something the team can hand someone on Monday.

Morning
Pick the Direction
Revisit the directions from Day 1. Which one holds up? What needs to shift? The goal is a single direction the room can commit to — not consensus on everything, just agreement on where to go.
Mid-Morning
What Should Lead the Story?
Platform, problem, outcome, or use case — the entry point determines what a buyer hears first. This is a real decision with real tradeoffs. We'll make it.
Midday
Write the Language
Turn the positioning direction into sentences a community bank executive can read without asking a follow-up question. Test multiple framings. Keep what's clear and credible.
Afternoon
Proof Points and Use Cases
What evidence backs the positioning? What client examples and outcomes make the story concrete enough to put in front of a buyer?
Late Afternoon
How the Story Carries Forward
Where does this show up first — sales conversations, the website, board decks? What changes Monday, what gets built over time?
Close
Next Steps
Who owns what. What Allison delivers in the synthesis. What the team starts using right away.

End of Day 2 Output

A clear positioning direction and a working message framework. Synthesis document to follow within 10 business days.
