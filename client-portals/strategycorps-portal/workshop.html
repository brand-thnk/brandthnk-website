Day 1: MIQ Positioning

Focus

Get the room aligned on what MonetizeIQ is trying to accomplish, where it fits in the product line, and what the positioning should be built around.

Opening
Setting Expectations
Each person answered: what's the one thing you want to walk out of this workshop with? Written first, then shared. That set the frame for the two days.
Morning
Challenge Alignment
True/false exercise to get a shared read on what's actually hard about MIQ: market interest exists but the story requires too much explanation, too many capabilities competing for attention, and no obvious first place to start.
Mid-Morning
What MIQ Should Accomplish First
Ranked six positioning priorities against each other: Earn Attention, Fast Comprehension, Credible Right to Win, Commercial Value, Clear Opening for sales, Logical Expansion path. The group decided which two to lead with.
Midday
Current Value Chain
Mapped out how the product line stacks: CheckingScore as the diagnostic layer, BaZing as execution, BaZing Biz as market expansion. Where does MIQ fit in relation to all of that?
Afternoon
Where Does MIQ Fit?
Three possible positioning architectures on the table: Use Case ("XYZ powered by..."), Platform ("Our platform now includes XYZ"), or Category ("Introducing XYZ, from the team that brought you..."). The group worked through which framing sets up the right conversation with bankers.
Late Afternoon
Box Edge Exercise
Applied the Box Edge framework to MIQ directly. Name the box (what category should a customer place you in?), define the edge (once you're in the right box, what makes you better?), then the 2-sentence test. Multiple drafts, tested against the room.
Close
Retro
What was one thing that stood out? Written, then shared. The answers shaped how Day 2 opened.

End of Day 1 Output

Challenge alignment, positioning priority decision, initial Box Edge drafts, and a decision record the group could walk into Day 2 with.

Day 2: Brand Architecture

Focus

Zoom out from MIQ to the full company story. How do BaZing, MonetizeIQ, and StrategyCorps connect — and how should that show up when someone lands on the site?

Opening
Homework + Retro
Two questions to open: briefly describe the best relationship in your life. Then share what your "one thing to get out of the workshop" was. The relationship question seeded the discussion about how StrategyCorps talks about what it builds.
Morning
Sample Site Walk-Through
30 minutes reviewing the draft StrategyCorps site Allison built. Participants walked through independently, made notes, and emailed observations to the group. The feedback from this session fed directly into the synthesis.
Mid-Morning
Brand Architecture Level Set
Framed how the product line actually reads: StrategyCorps brings 24 years of profitability work starting with CheckingScore. MonetizeIQ is an expression of that DNA. BaZing is the proof layer — not the old product being succeeded by a new one. MIQ is the expansion layer. The open question: is the identity to lean into StrategyCorps, BaZing, or MonetizeIQ?
Afternoon
Next Actions
Four deliverables defined: Brand and Product Architecture (how BaZing, MonetizeIQ, and StrategyCorps connect), Positioning and Messaging (positioning language and core message themes), GTM Playbook (actionable guidance for applying the new story), and Clearer Company Story (a unified narrative for StrategyCorps).

End of Day 2 Output

Site feedback collected, brand architecture framed, four synthesis deliverables scoped. Allison took it from there.
