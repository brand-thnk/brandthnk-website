Objective

What is the first line that "removed" people remember when engaging with our messaging?

Positioning Concepts

"We are a portfolio performance platform for community FIs that can add $1M of NII per $1B AUM." (Missing the proof — added at end of sentence.)
"Unlike product performance tools that stop at the diagnosis, we benchmark your product portfolio against 25 years of peer data and hand your team signals based on your own data, distilled into cohorts and offers. We make this actionable end-to-end, including the pricing, packaging and rollout plan."
Reworked: "We're a fee income growth platform for community banks and credit unions. Unlike analytics that stop at the diagnosis, we benchmark your portfolio against 25 years of peer data and deliver the pricing and packaging plan with it, so you add roughly $1M in annual fee income per $1B in assets."
"Imagine Zafin, Segment, Kasasa and Delta Sky Miles having a baby — that's us."

Concept Feedback

Likes the down-to-earth language — very much his tone.
Not clear if the box edge fully lands (the "unlike/competitors" part) — what's the role of proof ($, ecosystem integrations)?
Likes how it brings solutions together but doesn't feel transcending yet.
Missing the WIIFY — the what, what it replaces, category, ready for what's next.

Workshop Goal (as Mike framed it)

Determine general positioning of StrategyCorps with MonetizeIQ and historical solutions (CheckingScore, Bezing, BezingBiz) as a strategic solution expansion — not a "pivot" or "change" — that:

  • Makes sense to bankers and minimizes perceived new risk (not an old company with a startup division)
  • Is believable to the investment community — capitalize Bezing/BezingBiz earnings at SaaS multiples, not subscription multiples (12.5x EBITDA)

Day 2 Feedback

Good start on landing pages — much better than current MonetizeIQ pages.
Lean heavily toward Gain Attention for marketing. Fast Comprehension is difficult given all of MonetizeIQ's moving parts.
Need to work on Fast Comprehension in sales pitches — focus on what parts of MIQ solve the biggest/most immediate issues. Don't overwhelm bankers with all capabilities.
The SC System needs work — not connected or unified enough for the valuation capitalization objective.
Likes that it focuses on what MonetizeIQ does for an FI, not what MonetizeIQ is (technical/mechanics).
Doesn't like "enterprise" or "end-to-end" — bankers don't think or buy that way. Core processors use these terms and SC shouldn't be grouped with them.
Needs to be about solving real issues by versatility — a modular solver of modern banking issues AND an opportunity identifier and proven solution deliverer.
Less is more.
The proven part is only anecdotal — need to find "typical" ROI numbers and later update with actual ones, plus a few banker quotes/references to start the buyer's journey.

Key framing: A modular solver of modern banking issues — and an opportunity identifier with a proven solution delivery track record.
