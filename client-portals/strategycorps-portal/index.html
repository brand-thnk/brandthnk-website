Phase 1: Pre-Work

Objective

Gather the context and input needed to make the workshop productive from hour one. Understand the current business, products, and how leadership thinks about the story today.

Activities

  • Intake conversations with Vince and key leadership
  • Review of existing materials (pitch decks, website, sales collateral)
  • Prep brief sent to workshop participants
  • Allison orientation on BaZing and MonetizeIQ product lines

Deliverable

Workshop prep brief and orientation document for all participants.

Phase 2: Leadership Workshop

Objective

Bring the leadership team together to clarify how StrategyCorps should talk about the business, where it's headed, and how BaZing and MonetizeIQ fit into a single coherent story.

Activities

  • Day 1: Current state, company story, product integration, banker-friendly language
  • Day 2: Positioning direction selection, message framework, proof points, next steps
  • Facilitated working sessions with leadership team
  • Real-time synthesis and direction setting

Deliverable

Shared positioning direction and working message framework by end of Day 2.

Phase 3: Synthesis (Current)

Objective

Turn workshop outputs into a durable, usable positioning foundation the team can put to work immediately in board conversations, sales discussions, and marketing.

Activities

  • Synthesis write-up from workshop notes and session outputs
  • Clear company story documentation
  • BaZing and MonetizeIQ narrative integration
  • Message framework draft with banker-friendly language
  • Guidance for sales and marketing application

Deliverable

Final synthesis document with positioning direction, message framework, and practical guidance. Expected by Aug 15.
