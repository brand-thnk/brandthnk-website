Pre-Work Materials

Context and preparation ahead of the positioning workshop

Why We're Here Complete

StrategyCorps has a strong existing business and a bigger growth story taking shape. The challenge is less about vision and more about clarity: how to describe the company, the offering set, and the value in a way that helps leadership, sales, and marketing all tell the same story.

This workshop brings the leadership team together to close that gap — not to invent a new direction, but to get sharp about the one that's already there.

The Three Questions

What we're trying to answer

These three questions frame everything we'll work through together.

1. How should StrategyCorps describe what it is today?

2. How should BaZing and MonetizeIQ fit together in the story?

3. What is the clearest, most banker-friendly way to talk about the value going forward?

What We'll Produce

  • A clearer company story
  • A clearer way to talk about BaZing and MonetizeIQ together
  • Draft positioning language and message themes
  • Priority problems, outcomes, and use cases to lead with
  • Guidance for how the story should show up in sales and marketing

Pre-Work for Participants

Each participant reflected on these before Day 1. The answers shaped how the first session opened.

Individual Reflection Questions

  • How would you describe StrategyCorps to someone who's never heard of it? (in your own words, not from the website)
  • What's the thing bankers understand most clearly about what we do?
  • What's the thing that confuses them most?
  • How do you explain BaZing? How do you explain MonetizeIQ? How do you explain how they connect?
  • What are the 2-3 outcomes we deliver that matter most to a community bank?
  • Where does the story feel clearest today? Where does it feel muddiest?

Materials Reviewed Ahead of Workshop

  • Current website and positioning language
  • Recent pitch decks and sales materials
  • BaZing and MonetizeIQ product overviews
  • Representative client case examples

What Success Looks Like

By the end of the engagement, the leadership team should be able to:

  • Describe StrategyCorps in one clear sentence — without the product catalog
  • Explain how BaZing and MonetizeIQ fit into one integrated story
  • Walk into a board conversation or sales call with consistent, confident language
  • Hand marketing a foundation to build from — not start over

One thing to be clear about

The point isn't to invent something. The story is already there — we're just making it writable.
