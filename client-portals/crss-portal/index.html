Phase 1: Pre-Work (Current)

Objective

Establish an accurate, agreed-upon product truth baseline before any positioning or messaging decisions are made. The team must correct and approve this document before workshop proceeds.

Activities

  • Intake conversations with CRSS team
  • Review of existing marketing materials, pitch decks, and competitive collateral
  • Working product-truth summary delivered (Aug 11)
  • Team validation of factual claims, capability status, and technical boundaries
  • Corrections incorporated into approved baseline

Deliverable

Approved product-truth baseline: factual foundation for all positioning and messaging work that follows.

Phase 2: Positioning Workshop

Objective

Bring the CRSS leadership team together to choose the strongest commercial position for TracePipe — not a feature inventory, but a single reason-to-buy that the market can hold onto.

Activities

  • Open with agreed-upon product truth (no revisiting the brief)
  • Evaluate the 4 commercial hypotheses: workflow capture, portability, operational control, efficiency
  • Define the primary buyer and their most consequential problem
  • Select a positioning direction and test messaging against real buyer language
  • Identify proof points and claims that hold up to scrutiny
  • Draft message framework by end of session

Deliverable

Chosen positioning direction and working message framework approved by CRSS leadership.

Phase 3: Synthesis & Messaging

Objective

Turn workshop outputs into a positioning foundation the team can put to work immediately — in sales conversations, pitch decks, website copy, and launch materials.

Activities

  • Synthesis write-up from workshop outputs
  • Positioning statement and message hierarchy
  • Buyer language and proof point documentation
  • Guidance for applying the position across sales and marketing contexts

Deliverable

Final positioning document with approved language, message framework, and application guidance.
