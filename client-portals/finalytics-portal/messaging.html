Section 1 — Positioning

Market Category — Frame of Reference
Website personalization platform.

Intelligence is the promise, but it is not a market category.

Competitive Alternatives — What They Would Use Instead
  1. 1.Login-gated personalization inside digital banking (Alkami, Personetics, and the personalization modules of incumbent platforms).
  2. 2.A website redesign from an agency or a CU-focused web vendor.
  3. 3.Enterprise martech bought out of fit — Example, Braze.
  4. 4.Doing nothing, and accepting that the public site is the same page for everyone.
Unique Finalytics Attributes
  • Recognition and decisioning from the first anonymous visit, before any login
  • In-session adaptation — the page changes while the visitor is on it
  • Abandoned application recovery: "You started an application. Want to finish?"
  • 29 live integrations, first anonymous click through to funded loan
  • Funded attribution — which visits became funded loans and deposits, not which converted
  • A control group on every client site, so lift is measured rather than asserted
  • Wasted-channel proof — the ability to show a client what is producing nothing
  • Managed delivery. Finalytics runs it; sits on top of the existing site and stack
Customer Segments — And How to Spot One
Primary
Community credit unions and community banks, roughly $500M–$5B in assets

Where the retail marketing leader owns the website and carries a budget under $150K.

You are in the right account when:
  • Marketing owns the unauthenticated website and IT does not gate changes to it
  • Growth targets are stated in funded loans, deposits or new members
  • The team is small enough that "we run it for you" is a relief rather than a threat
  • There is a live agency relationship consuming a meaningful share of the marketing budget
  • A redesign is being discussed, budgeted or regretted

Section 2 — Messaging

Boilerplate

Finalytics is the website personalization platform for marketing leaders who need to turn traffic into funded loans and deposits. Unlike personalization that switches on after login, we adapt and run your site from the first anonymous click, increasing completed applications by 4.5x.

Short

Finalytics is the website personalization platform for community banks and credit unions. Most personalization starts after login, which means it never touches most of the traffic to your public site. Finalytics recognizes and adapts your website to every visitor from their first anonymous click, recovering abandoned applications, and tracing the result all the way to funded loans and deposits.

Long
With Finalytics, you'll never meet a stranger.

You have spent years learning about who you serve, but then have to show everyone the same website because most of your traffic is anonymous.

Finalytics works where loan and deposit growth actually starts: your public website.

What it does. Finalytics recognizes each visitor from their first anonymous click and adapts the site to them while they are on it and if someone abandons an application, Finalytics brings them back. And through 29 live integrations, it follows the result past the click and past the submission, to the funded loan or the funded deposit account.

Why it is different. Most personalization in this industry switches on after login. That serves your existing audience, but misses everyone else. The other alternative is a website redesign, which changes how the site looks without changing what any individual visitor sees. Finalytics adapts the site you already have, on top of the technology stack you already bought, to help you drive ROI and growth.

How you know it works. A control group runs on every client site, so we know for certain that clients running Finalytics saw 4.5x more completed applications than their control group on the same site. Finalytics also shows you what is not working: the channels with spend but without funded results.

Who runs it. Finalytics does. We know you have a small team with big goals and expectations!

Value Points by Persona
Persona A — Primary Buyer

CMO / VP of Marketing

Target Segment

Community bank or credit union, $500M–$5B assets

Buyer Persona

Owns the website, the brand and the agency relationship. Carries a growth number they do not fully control and cannot always prove they influenced.

Problem Space

Traffic exists, but funded growth does not follow. The reporting stops at form submission, so marketing's contribution is asserted rather than demonstrated.

Current Solutions

Agency-led redesigns, campaign tools, analytics dashboards, digital banking personalization they do not control.

Persona B

CEO / Chief Retail or Growth Officer

Buyer Persona

Owns the growth plan. Cares about funded loans, deposits and products per member. Skeptical of martech claims and has been burned by at least one.

Problem Space

Digital is the primary acquisition channel and the least measurable one. Branch economics are under pressure; the digital front door has to carry more.

Current Solutions

Core and digital banking vendors, agency partners, an internal digital roadmap.

Section 3 — Objection Resolution

"We already do personalization — it's in our digital banking platform."
That reaches people who already joined. It cannot see the stranger on your homepage deciding whether to apply. We start at the first anonymous click, before login, which is where new members and new loans come from.
"We'll need a new website first."
A redesign changes how the site looks to everyone, but it does not change what any one visitor sees. We adapt and run the site you have, to the person in front of it, and we can show you the lift against a control group on your own traffic.
"Our platform does everything — personalization is a module." (Alkami, MX, Salesforce)
Full-suite platforms are built for institutions with the team to run them. Every community FI marketer we meet has a story about enterprise software bought out of fit and never fully deployed. We are built for community financial institutions, not adapted for them, and we run it for you.
